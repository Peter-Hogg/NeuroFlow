# Code of Conduct

NeuroFlow follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

Project participants must provide a welcoming, harassment-free environment,
use inclusive language, accept constructive feedback, and prioritize community
safety. Unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to
peterhogg2006@gmail.com. Reports will be reviewed promptly and confidentially;
maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when necessary.
